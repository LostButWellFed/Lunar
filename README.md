# Lunar · Lost, But Well Fed

A 1979-style lunar lander. Land softly next to the street food. A soft landing refuels you ("well fed"). Run out of fuel and you're "Lost."

**Play:** https://lostbutwellfed.github.io/Lunar/

## How it plays
- Every landing or crash sends you to the next stop: Taco Stand, Milk Tea Stand, Papas Cart, then round again.
- Two worlds: Luna Taquera (grey moon) and Milk Tea Nebula (caramel dust, lighter gravity).
- Landing assist: let go of the rotate buttons and the lander levels itself.

## Controls
- Phone: two rotate buttons (left) and THRUST (right)
- Desktop: ← → rotate, ↑ or Space thrust, M sound

## Scoring
- Soft landing: 50 × pad multiplier, +300 fuel
- Hard landing: 15 × pad multiplier, +180 fuel
- Crash: −100 fuel
- Multipliers (x2, x3, x5) depend on how narrow the pad is, never on the stand.
- Your personal best is saved on your own device. No leaderboard.

## Edit the game
Open `game.js`. Everything you'd want to change is in the CONFIG block at the top: stands, landing lines, the order of stops, worlds, gravity and physics.

## Files
All files sit at the top level of the repo, with no folders. Upload every file.
